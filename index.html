The idea

Objects with a sense of place.

Serandib is a home brand inspired by Sri Lanka: its animals, ornament, colour, craft traditions and the easy richness of everyday life.

Tusker is the first collection — a series of statement pillows designed to feel at home on a modern sofa while carrying a distinctly Sri Lankan point of view.
